The work

Research in short

Cognitive Drive Architecture treats drive not as a personality trait but as a state that comes from how a few inner parts line up. The Law of Primode and Flexion Dynamics gives six variables and a computable equation that predicts when effort starts, holds or fails. My work also reaches into education, mental health, time and behaviour, and computation.
