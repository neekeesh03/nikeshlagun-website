Biography

Today

Author, researcher and founder of Lagun Lab, an independent research organization. Studying at Beihang University. Based between Kathmandu and Hangzhou.

Nikesh Lagun (b. October 24, 2005) is a Nepalese independent researcher and cognitive systems theorist. He is the founder of Cognitive Drive Architecture (CDA), a proposed structural field within cognitive psychology that models Drive and cognitive effort as an emergent property of real-time system configuration.

His foundational contribution to the field is Lagunian Dynamics, a first-principles theory that formally models the mechanics of Drive through six interacting system variables, spanning the domains of Ignition, Tension, and Flux.

His academic work extends across cognitive science, educational research, systems theory, productivity science, and applied control theory in psychology. In addition to his research, Lagun is also active as an acoustic singer-songwriter, visual artist, and public communicator on cognitive performance and resilience.

Academic and Research Work

Cognitive Drive Architecture (CDA)

CDA is a proposed structural field that redefines Drive not as a trait, but as a configurational state governed by formal interactions between cognitive variables.

  • The Law of Primode and Flexion Dynamics
  • Six system variables modeling Drive mechanics
  • A mathematically computable Drive equation predictive of performance outcomes
