Contact the Cognitive Drive Architecture Project

Purpose of Contact

This site represents an evolving field of inquiry: Cognitive Drive Architecture (CDA). It is maintained by Nikesh Lagun, independent researcher and founder of CDA.

  • Propose academic collaboration
  • Request interviews or field commentary related to cognitive effort, Drive, productivity, or systems theory
  • Discuss potential applications of CDA in education, cognitive science, or applied research
  • Inquire about public speaking engagements aligned with advancing CDA
  • Provide serious scholarly feedback on the evolving body of work

Please do not use this form for:

  • Commercial marketing proposals
  • General productivity coaching requests
  • Unrelated business partnerships

The goal of this project is to contribute to scientific understanding and public knowledge.All communications will be reviewed with this priority in mind.

Response Time

All inquiries are reviewed directly by Nikesh Lagun. Due to the independent and research-focused nature of this project, response time may vary. Priority is given to academic collaborations, media inquiries, and substantive discussions of CDA and its related models.
