Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. You can try Lagomo without creating an account. You can make one later if you want to save things.

Who can use it?

Anyone aged 16 or older, or the minimum age where you live if that is higher.

Is it based on real research?

Yes. It is designed around my research on why starting is hard. It is a tool for everyday use, not a medical or diagnostic product.

When does it launch?

The first week of 2027. Join the waitlist and I will email you when it opens.

Will anyone see what I'm putting off?

Only a friend you choose. There is no public feed.
