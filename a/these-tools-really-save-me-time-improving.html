These Tools Really Save Me Time: Improving Efficiency and Reducing Stress.

By Nikesh Lagun

Have you ever felt overwhelmed by your to-do list?

I certainly have.

Sometimes I feel like giving up.

Small tasks like making presentations used to consume hours of my time, often leading me to pull all night just to meet the deadline. After months of grappling with these challenges, I finally discovered how productivity tools could transform my approach to work. These productivity apps and software really help one to be more productive.

Productivity applications can reduce effort by an average of 60%, saving 3.6 hours per week.

Steve Jobs once said, “You cannot mandate productivity; you must provide the tools to let people become their best.”

Mac is regarded as having more productive tools built than any other technology.

It is because the first productivity tool can be traced back to the development of the Apple Macintosh in the early 1980s. Following the release of the Macintosh, the user experience for computers changed dramatically, thereby spurring many productivity tool developments.

Here are several resources that help to improve productivity, reduce effort, and improve efficiency.

Notion

Notion improves productivity by unifying notes, teamwork, and project management into a single tool.

Managing your content is more direct with the help of databases, kanban boards, calendars, and templates. Moreover, it can additionally act as a second brain in addition to its main features.

Amie

The team and individuals using Amie find their task management and scheduling easier. This application provides users the ability to access numerous features, such as task lists, organization of the calendar, and an individual CRM system, effectively collected in a single location.

This application provides a very valuable resource for tracking deadlines and preventing any careless mistakes.

ClickUp

ClickUp aims to improve team collaboration and simplify project management.

Elevated levels of sustainable productivity need a combination of task automation, time tracking, flexible dashboards, and the capacity to set and realize goals.

Forest

The Forest app enables users to prioritize their work while also adding to environmental sustainability.

Using this strategy, you will manage to isolate your unique focus period and boost productivity.

Perplexity AI

The search engine Perplexity makes use of AI technology to deliver users with fast and accurate responses for their search inquiries.

You can literally save your time instead of googling every single question.

Many more productivity tools can ease your life and daily activities.

As mentioned earlier, those are some of my favorites; I also use them for my tasks.

The other resources are:

  • ChatGPT

  • Quillbot

  • Todoist

  • Superlist

  • Readwise

  • Evernote

  • Google Calendar, etc.

If you’re using other tools other than this, let me know.
