Experimental Week

Hey buddy,

Are you doing great? Previously I wrote about the Atomic Habits by James Clear and mentioned some of my habits too. If you still need to catch up, here's the link.

After reading the book by James Clear, atomic habits changed my perspective, mainly the 1% rule has motivated me to create a new habit. So, this week is gonna be about the experimental habits. I call it an experimental week because I want to try new habits for a week, if I succeed in completing the challenge, of course, I will continue it.

Here are the few experiments I am trying to execute for productivity:

🏋️ Workouts:

It hasn't been so long since I started to work out. Before I used to work out for 15/20 minutes a day, it was awesome in the beginning but that ratio can't fit for a further time frame so I am going to increase my workout to 30 minutes a day and apply the 1% rule to increase up to 1/2 hours a day.

🧘‍♂️ Meditation:

Actually, I tried meditating for a week, but because of various kinds of distractions, I couldn't help but quit myself. But I want to continue it so this experimental week will be the perfect time to reinitiate it. Firstly I will try for at least 10 minutes then eventually increase by 2 minutes each day again applying the 1% rule for forming good and productive habits.

👨‍💻 Monk Mode:

I tried this too for not so long. I did break those habits. Now, again I will try it once more. If you don't know what monk mode is then it's basically to increase focus and avoid distractions like social media notifications, a docile brain for utilizing dopamine surges, no caffeine, meditations, workout, and productive life. I will try my best for a week and if I succeed will continue for 3 months take a break for a couple of days and restart it. If I fail then, I will restart it again.

⏳ Prioritizing:

Prioritizing things according to the urgency sucks for me. It's like trying to tame myself but it has its own significance for productivity. Using a block system, prioritizing stuff like newsletters, and other things can help in my case. I will also try making my timetable for a week so that I can be properly organized. Yeah, I will try this.

These are the topics I want to implement in my daily routine as an experimental week. It's cool to try these by yourself too. You don't have to implement the same things as I have mentioned instead you can apply other habits that interest you. Let me know if you'd love to join me in this experimental week, and we will figure it out together.

Have a great week!

Nikesh Lagun

✨ My Top Pick for This Week

🎬 Video: Yes, I sure was encouraged by Iman Gadzhi’s video. I intend to attempt the test for one week and gradually add more time. He describes the monk mode as a period of immersion when one is involved committed and emphatically locked in. Alcohol is not included, it mentions no use of wheat, 30 minutes of exercise a day, and 10 minutes of meditation. Other possible variables include prayer, how many minutes spend on their phone, or if they are sticking to a diet. The monk mode protocol can be changed according to the requirements of the corresponding individual.

📖 Book: "Rich Dad Poor Dad", a best-selling book by Robert T. Kiyosaki contrasts the financial philosophies of Kiyosaki's two father figures. Poor Dad values traditional education and job security, while Rich Dad promotes financial literacy, investing, and entrepreneurship. The book emphasizes making money work for you through assets and passive income. It encourages breaking away from the conventional mindset of working for money.
