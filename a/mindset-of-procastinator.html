Mindset of Procrastinator

By Nikesh Lagun

Hey friends,

I want to share a morsel of procrastination this week – If you want to learn to tackle procrastination, click here.

Well, today will be so much fun, as we will discuss how to understand procrastination. Yes, the topic is quite appealing since the majority of people deal with procrastination in one way or the other. Some people put their errands off until the last minute and one may wish to know what is going on in that head of theirs. Human brains and their working can be described through the following questions: Why do people procrastinate and what can this tell about human brains?

Thus, Tim Urban speculated something. He considered perhaps the executives or the brains of procrastinators were wired differently from those of the non-procrastinate types. Suppose there is an average or non-procrastinating person and let's compare the outcome with a procrastinating person. They have something there called the Rational Decision-Maker in both brains. Now this is where it gets really exciting – in the head of a procrastinator, there is another guy who is actually called the Instant Gratification Monkey.

Now, this monkey wants to mend the procrastinators in how they act as far as gaming is concerned. Perfect for an organizational setting, The Rational Decision-Maker is all about efficiency. The Instant Gratification Monkey has no regard for any of that though. It wants to do things that are not troublesome or unpleasant from the given scenarios, even if those are not helpful in the long run. That is why the Monkey might pull us toward doing something that we should not be doing such as browsing for hours on social media, munching on snacks continuously, or watching one funny video after another.

🙉 The Instant Gratification Monkey

With reference to the film or show of your choice, explain how the main character’s experiences exemplify the idea of living in the moment.”

The Monkey has no time frame, past or future, but only concerns itself with the current time. Where the Rational Decision-Maker can know everything, including the future, and where he is a player aware of the situation on a large scale, the Monkey only wants to have a scoop of this particular lover’s ice cream right now. This mindset is good for animals that are alive minute by minute. But as for us, the humans and specifically the civilized ones like the ones in this story, we have to look into the future and make preparations on how to contain ourselves for the long future.

⚔️ The Conflict and the Dark

This category consists of works that contain conflict between good and evil, which is represented through characters with evil personalities and intentions.

This puts procrastinators in a rather cruel spot where one part of their mind is logical and disciplined, or at least trying to be, while the other part is impatient and hedonistic. This perpetual conflict takes one to the Dark Playground; where enjoyable activities are performed during undesirable periods resulting in guilt, anxiety, and even dread since the fun is patently unwarranted.

🧟 Opening of the door of the Panic Monster

Luckily, procrastinators have a kind of savior: it is referring to the Panic Monster. This creature wakes up when targets are near or when there are grand consequences to deals. The panic monster chases away the Instant gratification monkey and allows the Rational decision-maker to return and produce short, powerful bursts of work.

⚠️ Long-Term Procrastination Leads to Serious Consequences

Failures due to Long Term Procrastination are grave since they lead to dire consequences resulting ultimately in death in some circumstances.

Laziness is not always in postponing such things to the last moment. It only shows that when procrastination is made as a person’s lifestyle, the impacts of the said behavior are not only limited to situations where important deadlines are due but can be disastrous even when there is no due date. Career progress, health, relationships, self-improvement – all these spheres may become an adverse effect of this tendency called procrastination. No timeframe alarms the Panic Monster, thus prolonging procrastination and enabling people to remain unhappy and remorseful for days, weeks, months, or even years.

🗒 Life Calendar

A very creative way of visualizing life is with the use of the “Life Calendar”.

Explaining how the Life Calendar is a new approach to thinking about each of the 90 years in a person’s life reduced to weeks. Calculating how many weeks are left can be a real eye-opener, especially, if one sees how many weeks are already gone. It reminds us to fight procrastination and concentrate on things that are of most value in our lives.

🚀 Take Action Against Procrastination

In conclusion, one must admit that the problem of Instant Gratification Monkey exists in every person and should be admitted and fought. Awareness when it comes to dealing with our internal battles and our schedule is the beginning of a solution to procrastination. I assure you the earlier we commence working on the process of freeing ourselves from procrastination the better it is for us. Perhaps not today but surely in the near future.

💭 Quote of the week

Procrastination is the thief of time, collar him." - Charles Dickens

Have a great week!
