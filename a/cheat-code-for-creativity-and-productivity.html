Cheat Code for Creativity and Productivity

By Nikesh Lagun

Share

Post on X ↗LinkedIn ↗Copy link ↗

Hey amigo,

Are you doing well?

Previously, I delved into the pool of my past college days, highlighting St. Ignatius Day; be grateful for what you have.

While I was going through the Ikigai; I learned a great lesson for productivity. One can be productive only if, s/he can go with the flow. You might have experienced a lot of these things.

When you sit down for a math problem, you feel boredom, you try to do two things; either you struggle or give up. But, when you solve the problem and head for the next, you won’t feel even an inch of boredom because of the flow. Once you enter the state of purity flow, nothing can stop you, despite distractions.

For instance, I write songs too: a love song. While I was busy thinking about a song, though, I had no idea of even a word for my song—my mind was passive for words and melody. As soon as I got a perfect word for my melody, I sat down and started writing phrases for my song, holding a guitar. Playing guitar for a few minutes with a warm-up, I got into a flow; a flow of creativity and productivity. That was my comfort zone for several hours.

The same can be applied to the studies too. When you feel, you're being distracted, go wash your face, take a quick nap, walk, or meditate for a few minutes. This not only makes you feel better but refreshes your mind and scraps your unwanted thoughts.

You can use the Pomodoro Technique for better concentration too. Getting distracted due to social media is a burning issue for today’s children.

A great piece of advice for you to overcome distraction is protecting your Time. Protecting time means allocating time for your most important task. Using a To-Do List can help you with it. While protecting your time don't get distracted even for a second, if you feel you're being distracted try Morita’s therapy mantra; ask yourself what you should be doing right now; In that meantime don’t try multitasking—Multi-tasking decreases productivity.

Getting into flow is not an easy task to do. You need to tackle various monotony to flow with the task you want to complete.

You get bored easily when you are dealing with the easy jobs. For example, if you're playing a game that is of an easy level, you get bored easily. But playing in the beast or boss mode makes you excited and thrilled to win the game because of the sudden spikes of dopamine surge. So, challenging oneself with difficult levels such as games can help you.

The other thing is the atmosphere—natural flora helps you feel motivated and productive. Use Parkinon’s law for a greater deal.

Use these techniques for better results. Remember, Rome was not built in a day, so make sure to try until you master the technique.

Have a great week!

Nikesh Lagun
