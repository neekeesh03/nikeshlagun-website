Tame the Procrastination Monster: 10 Strategies for Students to Boost Productivity

By Nikesh Lagun

Hello, friends!

Isn’t procrastination a pain?

One can face it in school, work, or even handling small tasks. This might be because of stress, hangovers, and long-term deadlines.

Here’s the scoop on procrastination: Late in the day, you fail to perform a task you are supposed to carry out, knowing very well that the consequences are grave. For instance, why do we fit to be in that dreadful movie? Why do we decide to produce such a film? Well, a few theories are floating around:

Temporal Motivation Theory (TMT):

If there is no speech in the present for choosing an action, we are not likely to do it. As we can see from the approach, the more time one wastes, the more unreasonable it is for him to do the specific task.

Self-Regulation Theory:

This one is that when we are unable to adequately manage our aims and drives, then delay becomes a cycle.

Cognitive-Behavioral Theory:

Yes, the daily negative cognitions that spoil the show—fear of failure or the elusive perfect as if it is a race without a finish line.

Understanding these theories helps us figure out an effective scheme to tackle procrastination.

It’s as if it is in our hardwiring to defer things till they turn into emergencies.

But hey, don’t fuss about it!

Let me tell you that we have some tactics to fight this procrastination monster. This is how we are going to get this thing done, head-on!

Procrastination not only delays your work but also reduces your creativity and productivity.

Sometimes it is so alarming that when you delay things in school, at work, or wherever you are, things get messed up. You can lose track of time, deliver subpar work, get overwhelmed, and even stunt your advancement in your workplace.

Oh, come on—that’s not going to help your reputation, is it?

But how do we, as students, avoid procrastination?

  1. One method is to define objectives and have policies.

  2. You have to learn how to state your goal in a specific manner instead of swearing that you would “write a paper.”

  3. Try aiming more specifically; for instance, “I should be able to write the intro by Friday.”

  4. Try Parkinson’s Law.

See the difference?

Resources are also important, particularly time management, which is paramount. For instance, you can choose to follow the Pomodoro technique, whereby one will do the work for 25 minutes non-stop before taking a 5-minute break. It assists in scheduling your work and ensures that you do not get exhausted or overwhelmed.

So what do you say?

Let’s not just switch up the chalkboard or the way you teach your lessons! Get over the ick that you are going to fail and begin to think small instead. Split the workload into segments—the concept reduces the dread of the tasks in question.

Your workspace matters too!

Tidy it up and switch off that notification from a social media platform or anything unrelated to what you are doing. Believe me, it changes the situation a lot. Perhaps do not forget to ask for some reinforcement as well. Share with your friends what you want to achieve and let them know how you are brushing up from time to time.

It keeps you accountable!

However, you can simply reward yourself as you progress or use apps to remind you of the due dates.

In short, procrastination is such a pain when it comes to creativity, yet it is easier to tame when one knows the strategies to put in place and people to turn to.

Well, studies have shown that several tricks can seriously help you to get things done.

Such things as time management—your several tricks can gain something and complete it too. Oh, and by the way, practicing mindfulness can also assist you in being attentive and mastery over such a tendency of procrastination. To sum it up, readers learn that procrastination is indeed a burning issue that affects their performance at school or work.

But fear not; by seeking out why you procrastinate and testing out these effective techniques, you will be able to up your rate and achieve your goals.

The idea here is to learn how you manage and what strategies help you function properly in given circumstances.

Keep at it, folks!

Have a great week!
