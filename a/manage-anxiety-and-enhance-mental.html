Manage Anxiety and Enhance Mental Health

By Nikesh Lagun

Hey fellow,

It's been a while, isn’t it?

I bet you checked out my latest article about Ikigai, a book review.

I shared every topic of it as I had been through.

Do you know?

Roughly 301 million people—an estimated 4% of the global population—have an anxiety disorder. This is because of the work time frame, overtime, financial difficulties, etc.

This newsletter can be shorter than usual but will carry more value within.

Let me share with you a short technique that I learned while reading a book called ‘The Charisma Myth’.

Having anxiety is such a burden, it not only affects our workflow but also affects our mental health.

If you ever feel your anxiety is hitting you harder, then use this technique called ‘Transferring Responsibilities’. This not only makes you feel better, but also helps you to stay calm and focus on your target.

  1. Be comfortable – you can either sit or even lie—at least try to find a position where you can be comfortable and then close your eyes.

  2. Inhale for three or four deep breaths. For instance, when entering a room or when taking a deep breath, imagine that you are inhaling fresh air and that it is going all the way to the top of your head. Just, as you exhale, that air rushes through, taking away all those things one has to worry and be concerned about.

  3. Select something to hold on to—perhaps God, luck, the universe, or whatever one would wish to. It may be worthwhile to look at it in terms of energy, as with other categories on the website.

  4. Now for a moment, picture yourself and all those things that are so sickeningly heavy—today’s meeting, for example, or that conversation you have to have—and deposit it all on the nicely arranged shoulders of this kindly disposed entity.

Imagine shedding out all your responsibilities! How light you are when a burden is no longer a thing that you have to endure. It is fully managed, and you barely have to do anything. So what’s the idea? That you should just chill and catch glimpses of good things now and then?

This trick might not work at first but try it all the time when your breath rushes and your heart pounds harder.

Have a great week!
