Ultimate Focus and Productivity

By Nikesh Lagun

Hey nerds,

How has your week been?

Are you getting and collecting guts ready for the next strategy?

Earlier, I discussed Parkinson’s Law and mentioned that it was the best way to beat procrastination. If you haven’t gone through it, here’s the link.

While I was preparing for the test, I used a great technique called the Pomodoro Technique. At first, I thought it would just be a time-consuming technique, but eventually became my favorite technique for studying.

If you don’t know about Pomodoro, then let me explain to you.

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method, having a 25-minute focus time frame followed by a minute break. It does have a long break of 15 or 30 minutes only after 4 intervals.

Then what’s the benefit of using Pomodoro?

Not only cool technique for time management, but the Pomodoro Technique also improves focus, tackles procrastination, develops the habit of single-tasking rather than multitasking, reduces unnecessary fatigue, and many more.

There are many apps and websites free as well as paid for the Pomodoro technique to utilize. Some of the best I like are Toptal, Pomofocus, and Forest. Regarding the forest, it's cool among the friends. The time you focus can lead to planting trees, and sharing among friends about the number of trees planted is a great idea. Not only do Forest plant trees virtually, they plant real trees too.

So, if you try studying, then don’t forget to try the Pomodoro technique.

Have a great week!
