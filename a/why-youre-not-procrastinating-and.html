The Secret Reason You Can’t Start Anything

By Nikesh Lagun

The hidden cognitive blocks that stop you from getting started.

Share

Post on X ↗LinkedIn ↗Copy link ↗

Hey friends,

We’ve all been there, sitting with a task in front of us, knowing full well what needs to be done, yet still unable to take that first step. The clock ticks, the pressure builds, but somehow, we remain stuck. It’s frustrating, and at times, it can feel like we’re avoiding the work on purpose. But here’s the thing: procrastination doesn’t always start as a choice.

Procrastination feels emotional.

It feels like resistance. It feels like a lack of willpower. We tell ourselves, "Why can’t I just get started?" And sure, the feeling of being paralyzed or overwhelmed is real, but it doesn’t always come from a lack of motivation. Often, the root cause is far more structural than we realize.

But it often starts structurally.

When Primode fails, and your Cognitive Activation Potential (CAP) is low, the problem isn’t laziness; it’s ignition failure. These are two key components in how our minds decide to get things moving. Think of Primode as your internal switch, the threshold your brain has to cross before it can act. And CAP is the fuel, that burst of energy or motivation that drives the start of any task. When these systems are out of sync, nothing happens. You don’t just choose not to start. You literally haven’t reached the point where your system is ready to turn on.

When Primode fails and CAP is low, nothing begins.

It’s like trying to start a car with no gas. You know where you want to go, and you can see the road ahead, but the engine just won’t fire up. It’s not that you’re avoiding the task; it’s that you haven’t yet crossed that internal threshold of readiness.

You’re not avoiding. You just haven’t ignited.

This is a subtle but powerful shift in perspective. You aren’t procrastinating because you don’t care. You’re procrastinating because your internal systems haven’t aligned. Your brain simply hasn’t hit the "go" button yet. But here’s the good news: once you understand this, you can work with it. Just like a car needs a bit of a jumpstart when the battery’s low, your system can be primed to overcome that ignition failure.

So, the next time you find yourself stuck, remember, it's not about forcing yourself to act when you don't feel like it. It's about setting the right conditions so your internal "engine" can fire up. It's about adjusting your Primode and boosting your CAP to get you moving.

With a bit of practice and awareness, you can go from feeling stuck to taking action with ease. Just like that car, sometimes all it takes is a little nudge to get the engine running.

Wishing you a lighter, easier week ahead,

Nikesh Lagun
