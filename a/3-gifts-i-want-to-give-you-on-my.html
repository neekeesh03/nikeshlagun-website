3 gifts I want to give you on my 20th birthday.

By Nikesh Lagun

A small thank-you for everyone learning to start again.

In this articleToday’s my birthday 🎂a small gift, from me to youand something new is arriving soonone last thing

Share

Post on X ↗LinkedIn ↗Copy link ↗

Hey friends,

Sorry, it’s overwhelming to go through 2 issues a week, but

I had to because….

Today’s my birthday 🎂

But more than that, it feels like a pause, a small breath between everything that’s been building, breaking, and becoming.

Birthdays have a strange rhythm, don’t they?

They make you look back and forward at the same time.

You start to see the threads: the things that left, the ones that stayed, and the quiet changes that took years to happen.

This year has been one of those quiet changes.

the year that changed everything

CDA, Cognitive Drive Architecture, grew from an idea into a field.

Lagun’s Law. CTT. LTA.

The theories I built in silence finally found their voice.

But somewhere inside all that science, equations, and structure…

I rediscovered the reason I started at all:

the human pulse underneath it.

The part of us that just wants to move, create, and care, but sometimes can’t.

The part that still tries anyway.

When I began writing about Drive, I wasn’t just researching effort.

I was trying to understand why people with heart, vision, and desire still stall at the starting line.

And somehow, in that search, I found you.

The ones who read, who sent messages, who told me my words made them feel seen.

You stayed.

You believed in something that didn’t even have a name yet.

You reminded me that what I’m building isn’t about theory.

It’s about people.

a small gift, from me to you

So, on this birthday, I want to give something back.

I’m opening everything.

All of it, free for everyone.

And a fun fact? Switch ON was actually the week’s best-selling book in Amazon’s free cognitive psychology section:

Today, you can also grab a copy!!

No paywalls. No signup forms.

Just stories, science, and structure, shared openly.

Because if there’s one thing CDA has taught me, it’s this:

Drive isn’t something we own.

It’s something we share.

We ignite each other.

And sometimes, that spark comes just from knowing someone else out there is trying too.

and something new is arriving soon

Today, my first studio release goes live,

🎶 Parkhiraxu

It’s a song about waiting,

not the heavy, lonely kind,

but the quiet one that feels like breathing.

It’s about a love that doesn’t rush.

That just stays.

You wait, not because you have to,

but because some part of you already knows it’s worth it.

Time keeps trying to move you on.

But love doesn’t listen to time.

It has its own rhythm. Its own patience.

That’s what this song is about: that soft, stubborn promise that even if it

takes forever, you’ll still be there.

one last thing

If you’ve read this far

THANK YOU!!!

For being part of this strange, beautiful experiment of art and mind.

For walking with me as I tried to make sense of Drive, effort, and love.

For reminding me that even the most technical theories about cognition are, at their heart, about humans trying their best.

Here’s to another year of building things that feel alive,

and to the quiet courage it takes to stay.

With love always,

– Nikesh
