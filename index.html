A Field in Formation

I'm Nikesh Lagun, an independent researcher and theorist developing Cognitive Drive Architecture (CDA), a proposed structural field within cognitive psychology. CDA models Drive not as a fixed trait but as an emergent property of real-time system configuration, formalized through Lagunian Dynamics.
