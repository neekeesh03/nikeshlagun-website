Hi, I'm Nikesh

I turn hard research into simple help

I'm a researcher and author from Nepal, now studying at Beihang University in China. I began by asking a simple question: why do people who care and know what to do still struggle to start?

Since then I have written books, 49 essays and 24 research papers, and I'm building a gentle app. Everything here is meant to be useful first, and clear enough to share with a friend.

What I do

I study why it is hard to start and stay focused, then turn it into free books, essays and simple tools. The big idea: you are not lazy, your brain just needs an easier way in.

Try it now

A 15-second experiment on your own mind

No sign up. Look at your hand and see how your focus and starting power are working today.

What you will do

  • Look at your hand for 3 seconds
  • Silently pick a finger, but do not move it
  • Change your mind to another finger
  • Then actually lift one
  • Notice what happened

Your mind is not used to this odd little task, so you see how it really behaves under a bit of pressure, with no habit to hide behind.

The ideas behind it

Three reasons effort goes wrong

My research breaks effort into three simple parts. When one part slips, you feel stuck, even when you care.

1 · Starting

You can't begin

You want to, but your mind has not clicked on yet. A smaller first step usually helps.

2 · Staying

You can't stay focused

Your attention drifts or the task feels heavy. Short blocks and fewer open loops help.

3 · Steadiness

Some days are just off

Your energy goes up and down. Knowing this lets you plan around it instead of blaming yourself.
