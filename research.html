From intention to action

A simplified model of the states a person moves through. Not a medical model.

stateDiagram-v2
  direction LR
  state "Sustained action" as Sustained
  [*] --> Intention
  Intention --> Initiation: readiness passes the threshold
  Initiation --> Sustained: focus holds and friction stays low
  Intention --> Stuck: readiness too low
  Initiation --> Burnout: friction too high
  Initiation --> Drift: focus slips
  Sustained --> Drift: friction builds up
  Drift --> Initiation: reset and restart

Lagun's Law at a glance

The equation behind the theory, drawn as a flow. Drive = (Primode to the power of CAP, times Flexion) divided by (Anchory plus Grain), plus Slip.

flowchart LR
  P["Primode
readiness to start"] --> TOP["Push to start
Primode to the power of CAP, times Flexion"]
  C["CAP
push of willpower"] --> TOP
  F["Flexion
fit with the task"] --> TOP
  A["Anchory
focus grip"] --> BOT["Resistance
Anchory plus Grain"]
  G["Grain
inner friction"] --> BOT
  TOP --> DIV{{"push divided by resistance"}}
  BOT --> DIV
  DIV --> ADD(("plus Slip
wobble"))
  ADD --> D["Drive"]
