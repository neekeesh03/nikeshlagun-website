Unspoken Goodbye: the hardest goodbye is the one never spoken

A deeply emotional breakup novel about first love, heartbreak and the quiet strength it takes to let go.

Buy at Barnes & Noble ↗Buy on Amazon

About the book

He believed love would be simple. Late-night talks, shared promises, and a future unfolding together. But when her messages faded into silence and their bond dissolved without explanation, he was left with only questions, a torn notebook, and a heart full of echoes.

Through raw reflections, intimate moments, and the symbols of a river, a fading plant, and an unfinished diary, this story captures the pain of unanswered goodbyes and the beauty of healing when closure never comes.

For readers who love heartfelt romance, coming-of-age stories, and novels about love and loss.
