"What if the hardest goodbye is the one that is never spoken?"

The story

He believed love would be simple

Late-night talks, shared promises, and a future unfolding together. But when her messages faded into silence and their bond dissolved without explanation, he was left with only questions, a torn notebook, and a heart full of echoes.

Through raw reflections and intimate moments, this story captures the pain of unanswered goodbyes and the beauty of healing when closure never comes.

The river
