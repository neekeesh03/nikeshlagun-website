Inside the book

Six parts of your mind's drive engine

The book explains six simple parts. When one of them slips, starting or staying focused gets hard.

P

Primode

Your readiness to start

How ready your mind is to begin. When it is low, nothing starts, however much you care.

C

CAP

Your push of willpower

The extra push you can add. More push helps you start, but only up to a point.

F

Flexion

How well the task fits you

Whether the task suits your mind right now. A good fit keeps you going.

A

Anchory

Your focus grip

How well you can hold your attention on one thing without it drifting.

G

Grain

Inner friction

The resistance inside. The more friction, the more tiring every task feels.

S

Slip

Day-to-day wobble

The ups and downs in how you perform. Some wobble is normal.
