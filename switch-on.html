Switch On: Your mind's Drive engine

Are you stuck, burned out or overwhelmed no matter how motivated you feel? This handbook explains why traditional productivity hacks often fail and how a structural understanding of your Drive can change your habits for good.

Read free online ↗Download free PDF
SWITCH ONA User's Guide to Your Mind's Drive EngineNikesh Lagun

What you'll learn

  • The six variables of Lagunian Dynamics: Primode, CAP, Flexion, Anchory, Grain and Slip
  • How to diagnose what stalls you: start failure, load collapse, anchor fatigue and more
  • Practical tools, relatable examples and guided exercises to repair and maintain your Drive
  • Why stuckness is a fixable system problem, not a personal flaw
"You are not a machine. Treat yourself with the same kindness you would offer a close friend. When Drive collapses, it is not a moral failure. It is a system problem, and you now have the tools to tune and repair that system."
