Switch On: get your mind moving again

Stuck, burned out or overwhelmed even though you care? This free guide explains why the usual productivity hacks fail, and gives you simple things to try instead.

Read free online ↗Download free PDF

What you'll get

  • Why you can care about something and still not start it
  • A simple way to spot what is holding you back: starting, focus, or heavy tasks
  • Practical tools, real examples and short exercises
  • A kinder way to see yourself: stuck is fixable, not a flaw
"You are not a machine. Treat yourself with the same kindness you would offer a close friend. When your drive collapses, it is not a moral failure. It is a system problem, and you now have the tools to repair it."
