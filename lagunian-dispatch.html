Lagunian Dispatch

A weekly field newsletter on the structure of effort, focus, and cognitive performance

Introduction

Lagunian Dispatch is a weekly field newsletter written by Nikesh Lagun, founder of Cognitive Drive Architecture (CDA). It serves as an ongoing record of new insights from the evolving architecture of Drive, practical reflections on cognitive effort and focus, and theoretical notes from ongoing work on Lagunian Dynamics.

It is intended as a permanent thread in the formation of this field, recording what is being learned about the structure of effort in real time.

Subscribe to Lagunian Dispatch

Join a growing community of researchers, educators, and cognitive scientists following the development of Cognitive Drive Architecture.

Subscribers receive:

  • Theoretical essays on Drive and performance
  • Explorations of failure patterns and resilience strategies
  • Structural notes on applying CDA in work and life
  • Pre-publication excerpts of new papers and models
  • Reflections on the nature of sustained human effort

*You may unsubscribe at any time. The Dispatch is free and offered in the spirit of scientific inquiry.

The Purpose of Lagunian Dispatch

In any emerging scientific field, there must be a space where models can be explored before formal publication, structural insights can be shared with practitioners, and the architecture of the field itself can evolve in public view.

Lagunian Dispatch fulfills this function for Cognitive Drive Architecture. It is written in the hope that future scholars will read this trail of thought as the field matures.
