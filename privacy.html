Privacy, plainly

What happens to your email, and what this site does and does not do.

Last updated 3 November 2026

Who I am

I am Nikesh Lagun, an author and researcher based in Kathmandu, Nepal. If you have a question about anything here, write to lagunnikesh2064@gmail.com. A real person reads it.

What happens to your email

When you type your email into the free-book box or any newsletter box, it goes to Substack, the service that sends my newsletter, the Lagunian Dispatch. Substack emails you to confirm, and then you get my letters. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time. This website itself does not keep a copy of your email.

What this site saves on your device

One small note, and only if you unlock the free book: it remembers that you did, so you do not have to enter your email again. That is all. You can clear it by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Tracking

This site does not run analytics or advertising trackers of its own, and it does not use cookies to follow you around.

Other companies your browser talks to

To load the page, your browser may contact other services, and they can see your internet address when it does: Google Fonts for the text styles, Substack for newsletter images and sign-up, and jsDelivr for the diagram tool on the research pages. If you click a link to Yumpu, Zenodo, Spotify, YouTube, Amazon or similar, you leave this site and their own policies apply.

About the checks and tips

The hand check, the eye check and the tips are fun self-checks drawn from my research. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis or a replacement for a professional. Some ideas are published papers, some are early findings from my own notes, and some are my own methods. I label which is which where I can.

Corrections

If you spot a mistake, a broken link or a claim that needs a better source, tell me by email and I will fix it and note the change.
